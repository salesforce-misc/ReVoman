## Decisions that the brief does not determine (12)

These need a user-approved design choice; the brief requires that they be frozen but does not itself
supply enough bytes/rules to derive them safely.

1. Exact literal domain byte strings, envelope magic/version placement, maximum payload sizes, and
   canonical payload serializer for every signed domain.
2. The single private-key raw encoding (seed versus another representation), public-key file bytes,
   key-ID hash input, and exact OpenSSH-fingerprint construction bytes.
3. Complete canonical schema/key order/type/number/string escaping/caps for the three terminal
   payloads and every release/review/approval record.
4. Full argv, optionality, and canonical stdout records for `verify-design-approval` and
   `verify-fixed-range-review`; the brief fully lists only `verify-release-set` and describes, but
   does not enumerate, `verify-clearance-result` operands.
5. Design-approval, reviewer, administrator, bootstrap, and component-compromise trust-anchor
   identity/rotation/revocation rules and exact signed record formats.
6. Local setup-failure, collector-result, persistence-result, and validation-attempt field sets,
   dispositions, byte caps, and content-derived path formulas.
7. Exact owner/child liveness proof and semantic-child wait-status encodings, plus normal repeat
   validation result fields/status mapping beyond the specified status-`6` and status-`77` lines.
8. Recovery-disposition schema, sequence upper bound, digest/path derivation, current namespace
   observation bytes, and the complete status grammar for unchanged/pre-claim recovery.
9. The exhaustive clearance report-kind × mode × reason × trust-disposition nullability table;
   narrative constraints do not select every row or all required incident/remediation values.
10. Canonical component-compromise and partial-claim proof schemas, their independent replacement
    authority, and the precise provenance/remediation compatibility rules.
11. Exact post-build bootstrap approval schema plus executor FD map, environment allowlist, and
    allowed verifier-launch argv shape.
12. Test-infrastructure attestation schema and syscall-barrier notification/ack/fault grammar, and
    the complete native entry/payload/copier/signer ABI (mode strings, FD map, handoff/result bytes,
    bounds, and statuses).

Until these 12 decisions are approved and reflected in the design, the required fixture vectors can
only be speculative; production source or RED test implementation must not begin.
